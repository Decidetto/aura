Privacy & Data Security

In local mode, recordings are processed entirely offline on your machine and never leave your PC. In cloud mode, audio, the generated transcript, selected text (when selection editing is active), and custom dictionary terms are sent directly to your chosen provider over TLS with zero telemetry. API keys and history are encrypted locally with Windows DPAPI. Optional update checks run only on demand or when explicitly enabled.
